## 3. Design decisions

- **Graphite canvas, ivory documents, one copper accent.** Finance work is about documents, so the visual language is documents under precise light: ledgers, case files and registers. Copper marks only the primary action, highlighted items and progress.
- **Sans display type** (IBM Plex Sans 500, tight tracking) with **monospace for every identifier and amount** (Plex Mono, tabular figures). Both were already self-hosted.
- **Motion that explains rather than decorates:**
  - ledger rows settle and a copper line draws from deduction to resolution;
  - scattered records spring into one case file;
  - the stage spine fills as you scroll;
  - the register filters in place.

  None of it blocks reading or clicking, and all of it is off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **The claim register is the centrepiece of trust.** It shows the eight statuses, a partial approval, and the gap between "retailer approved" and "recovered". It is labelled as synthetic everywhere.
- **No published prices.** The fee structure is described in words and "recovery" is defined on the homepage, in the FAQ and in the Terms.

## 4. Content changes

- **Positioning:** everything about MAP enforcement has been replaced with retailer deduction investigation and recovery (see `STRATEGY.md`).
- **Location:** "operated from India" is now stated in the footer, on About, in the FAQ, in the Privacy Policy and in the Organization structured data. It had previously been removed.
- **Initial scope:** "Walmart supplier deductions" appears on the homepage, Services, Who We Help, About and in the FAQ. Other retailers are described as "only after validation".
- **Primary CTA everywhere:** "Discuss Your Deduction Backlog" → `/contact/`. The header uses the shorter "Discuss your backlog" because of the width available.

## 5. Founder decisions outstanding

1. **Founder identity on About.** No name or biography is published. A marked placeholder is in `src/pages/about.html` (an HTML comment, not visible). Please supply your name, role and a short factual background. I have not inferred any of it.
2. **Fee disclosure.** The site describes "a monthly managed-service fee plus a performance fee" but publishes neither the ~$1,000/month target nor the 20%. Decide whether to publish either.
3. **Secure record exchange.** The site says records are exchanged "through a secure method agreed with you after engagement". Choose and document the actual method (for example, a specific encrypted file-sharing service) so you can name it when asked.
4. **Analytics provider.** `main.js` already records `page_view`, `cta_click`, `form_start`, `form_submit`, `form_error` and `register_filter`, each with UTM and referrer attribution, into `window.dataLayer`. Nothing is sent anywhere until you pick a provider. Options: Vercel Web Analytics (enable it in the dashboard, then add its script) or Plausible. Update the Privacy Policy (§7) before turning either on.
5. **Form delivery.** The form still uses FormSubmit to email ayman@mapdeltahq.com. If FormSubmit was never activated, send one test enquiry and click the activation link in that inbox. Until then, visitors see a pre-filled email fallback.
6. **Reply-time commitment.** "We aim to reply within one US business day" was carried over from the previous site. Confirm you can keep to it.
7. **Correction commitment.** The trust section says "If we get something wrong, we tell you, correct the record and note what changed." Confirm this is how you will operate.
8. **Recoveries paid to the client.** The copy assumes retailers pay or credit the supplier directly and MapDelta never holds retailer funds. Confirm this.

## 7. Claims awaiting verification

Each of these is worded cautiously on the site. The founder should still confirm each one before relying on it in sales conversations.

- **Walmart validation.** Walmart is the initial operating scope, and the site says other retailers are taken on only after validation. Confirm the Walmart procedures (dispute process, evidence requirements, time limits) are documented.
- **Supplier system access.** The site says MapDelta submits disputes "through the retailer's process using access you grant". Confirm the access model with your first client.
- **Who does the work.** The site says "The founder leads every engagement" and that others are disclosed before they work on a case. Keep this true as the team grows.
- **Retention.** "Generally no longer than 24 months" for enquiries was carried over from the previous Privacy Policy.
- **Counsel review.** The Privacy Policy and Terms were both rewritten for the new service. Have counsel review them before relying on them.

## 8. Tests performed

See section 9 for the results.

- Built the site with `scripts/build.py` (no unfilled placeholders, 8 sitemap URLs).
- Screenshots of all 9 pages at 1440, 834 and 390 px, checking for horizontal overflow and console errors.
- Accessibility: axe-core 4.10 (WCAG 2.1 A/AA plus best practice) on every page at desktop and mobile widths.
- Keyboard: tab order through the header, menu, CTAs, register filters, FAQ and form; Escape closes the mobile menu.
- Reduced motion: every element visible in its final state, with no transitions.
- Form: validation errors, successful submission (with the FormSubmit endpoint stubbed so no email was sent), failure fallback, and the honeypot.
- Core Web Vitals proxy: Chromium with a slow-4G profile (1.6 Mbps, 150 ms RTT) and 4× CPU throttling, measuring LCP, CLS, event duration and transfer size.
- Internal link crawl across all pages.

## 10. Known limitations

- **Browsers.** Only Chromium is installed in this environment. Firefox and Safari were not tested. The CSS degrades gracefully, for example where `interpolate-size` isn't supported for FAQ animation, but should be checked on a real iPhone and in Safari.
- **INP.** It can only be measured from real-user field data. The figure reported here is the longest event duration recorded in a lab run.
- **Live verification after deploy.** The `trailingSlash` setting, security headers and `.vercelignore` behaviour should be confirmed on the live domain after the first deployment.
- **Share previews.** LinkedIn and email clients cache share previews, so the new OG image may take time to appear.
