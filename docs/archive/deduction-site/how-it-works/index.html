How It Works

Five stages. Two decisions that are always yours.

Every deduction we investigate moves through the same controlled sequence, from the first remittance line to verified cash or credit. You set the scope before work starts, and you approve every dispute before it reaches a retailer.

Discuss Your Deduction Backlog

The five stages in detail

01

Capture

We receive the deduction and payment information you authorise us to use, for the retailer and period agreed in the service agreement.

What happens

Deduction and remittance data is loaded into a case register. Each deduction gets a case reference, and nothing in scope is left unlisted.

What we need from you

Retailer deduction and remittance reports for the agreed period, exchanged through the secure method we agree after engagement.

What you receive

An intake register: every deduction in scope, with amount, date, type as reported and age.

02

Investigate

We classify each deduction and reconcile it against your payment and invoice records.

What happens

Deductions are grouped by type and matched to the invoices and remittances they relate to. Duplicates, repeated patterns and unexplained items are identified.

What we need from you

Invoice and payment records for the same period, and answers to occasional questions about how your accounts are kept.

What you receive

A classified, reconciled view of the backlog, with an early indication of where effort is likely to be worthwhile.

03

Validate

We match each case to its purchase orders, agreements and supporting documents, and assess whether the evidence supports a dispute.

What happens

Each case is marked potentially supportable, awaiting evidence, or valid. Valid deductions are closed with the reason recorded, not disputed.

What we need from you

Purchase orders, proof of delivery, carrier records and relevant agreements, such as promotional or allowance terms.

What you receive

An evidence assessment for each case, linked to the source documents it relies on.

04

Resolve

We prepare evidence-backed disputes for your approval. Once you approve, we submit them and manage the follow-up you have authorised.

What happens

Disputes are submitted through the retailer’s process within its time limits, then tracked until the retailer decides. Denials are recorded with their reasons.

What we need from you

Approval of each dispute, and access to the retailer’s supplier systems at the level the agreed work requires.

What you receive

A record of every submission and follow-up, and the retailer’s decision on each case.

05

Reconcile

We verify outcomes against what actually reached you. A case counts as recovered only when the cash is received or the credit is applied.

What happens

Retailer approvals are matched to later remittances and credits. Partial approvals stay open until the approved amount is realised.

What we need from you

Ongoing remittance data, so recoveries can be verified rather than assumed.

What you receive

A monthly report: cases by status, verified recoveries, open items and the recurring causes behind your deductions.

Where you decide

You stay in control of what is sent, and to whom.

  • 01

    Scope

    Which retailer, which period and how many records. Agreed in writing before anything is shared.

  • 02

    Each dispute

    Nothing is submitted to a retailer until you have reviewed and approved the case.

  • 03

    Whether to continue

    After a denial or a partial approval, we recommend a next step, and you decide whether to pursue it.
