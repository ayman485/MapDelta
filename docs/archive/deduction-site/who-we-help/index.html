Who We Help

Suppliers whose deductions have outgrown the time to investigate them.

MapDelta is built for consumer-goods suppliers that sell through large retailers, carry recurring deductions, and don’t have the internal capacity to investigate all of them.

Supplier categories

Where we focus.

  • Consumer-goods suppliers

    Branded products shipped to large retail chains on regular replenishment cycles, where deductions arrive with every remittance.

  • Food and beverage suppliers

    Frequent, high-volume shipments where shortage, pricing and promotional deductions can accumulate quickly.

  • Beauty and personal-care manufacturers

    Promotion-heavy programmes where allowance and pricing deductions need careful matching to agreed terms.

  • Household-product suppliers

    Broad assortments where many small deductions across many lines are easy to leave unexamined.

  • Pet-product brands

    Growing retail programmes where deduction volume has grown faster than finance capacity.

Situations

When suppliers usually call.

A backlog has built up

Months of deductions have been logged but not investigated, and some dispute windows are getting close.

The team is stretched

Accounts receivable is covering collections, cash application and deductions with the same few people.

The same deductions keep returning

Similar deductions recur, but nobody has had time to trace why or to dispute the ones that don’t hold up.

Fit

A typical fit, and when we’re not the right choice.

These are indications, not eligibility rules. If you’re unsure, describe your situation and we’ll tell you honestly.

Often a good fit

  • Recurring deductions from Walmart, our initial operating scope.
  • An unresolved backlog, or new deductions arriving faster than they can be investigated.
  • Records that exist (invoices, purchase orders, proof of delivery) even if they’re scattered.
  • A finance or AR team that wants the work done, not another tool to run.
  • Often companies of roughly $10M–$100M in revenue and 20–300 employees, though size alone doesn’t decide it.

Probably not the right choice yet

  • Your deductions come mainly from retailers outside our validated scope.
  • Deduction volume is low enough that your team already investigates every one.
  • You need legal representation or formal proceedings rather than operational recovery work.
  • Supporting records don’t exist and can’t be obtained.

Next step

There may be deductions worth investigating. Start with the records.
