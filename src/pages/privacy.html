The short version

  • Through this website we collect only what you type into the contact form, plus basic technical data our hosting provider records.
  • Financial records are never collected through this website. Client records are handled under a written service agreement.
  • Information may be processed in the United States and in other countries where we and our service providers operate.
  • We don’t sell personal information, use advertising trackers, or add enquirers to mailing lists.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how MapDelta (“we”, “us”) handles information when you visit our website or contact us, and how we approach information we process for clients. MapDelta provides managed retailer deduction investigation and recovery services (the “Services”).

If you are a client, your service agreement with MapDelta also governs how we handle the records we process for you. Where it conflicts with this policy, the service agreement controls.

2. Information from website enquiries

Information you give us

  • Contact form: your name, company, business email, job title, the retailers you supply, an optional approximate deduction range, and your description of the current problem.
  • Correspondence: anything you include when you email or speak with us.

Source information

When you send the contact form, it also includes how you reached our website: the referring website, if any, and campaign tags in the link you followed (for example, utm_source). This is held in your browser’s session storage until you close the tab, and is sent only with a form you choose to submit. We use it to understand which outreach is useful.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider records basic technical data when you visit, such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and the time of the request. It is used to operate and secure the website.

3. Client records

To provide the Services, clients share business records such as retailer deduction and remittance reports, invoices, purchase orders, proof of delivery and relevant agreements. These may include the names and business contact details of people at the client or the retailer.

We process client records only to provide the Services the client has asked for, within the scope set in the service agreement. Records are exchanged through a secure method agreed with the client after engagement, never through this website.

4. How we use information

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss whether the Services suit you.
  • To provide the Services to clients, as set out in their service agreements.
  • To understand which outreach and referral sources lead to enquiries.
  • To operate and secure our website and Services.
  • To meet legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

Sending an enquiry doesn’t subscribe you to anything. We won’t send you marketing email unless you ask us to.

5. How we share information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We share information only:

  • With service providers that help us run the business, such as website hosting (Vercel), form delivery (FormSubmit), email and document storage. They may use it only to provide their services to us.
  • With a retailer, when a client has approved a dispute and we submit it on the client’s behalf.
  • For legal reasons, when required by law or to protect our rights, our clients or others.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger or acquisition, subject to this policy.

6. Where information is processed

Information you send us, and client records we process, may be accessed and processed in the United States and in other countries where MapDelta and its service providers operate. For client records, the location, storage and access arrangements are agreed in the service agreement.

7. Cookies and measurement

Our website does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Fonts are served from our own website. Source information (section 2) is kept in session storage, not in a cookie, and is cleared when you close the tab.

If we add a website analytics provider, we will update this policy before doing so.

8. Retention

We keep enquiries and related correspondence for as long as needed to respond and for our business records, and generally no longer than 24 months after our last contact with you. Client records are kept for the length of the engagement and as set out in the service agreement, after which they are returned or deleted unless the law requires us to keep them.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, including access limited to the people doing the work, and encrypted connections for this website. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can’t guarantee absolute security. MapDelta does not currently hold a third-party security certification.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including California and other US states with privacy laws, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to get a copy, to ask us to correct or delete it, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. We don’t sell or share personal information for advertising.

To make a request, email ayman@mapdeltahq.com. We may need to verify your identity, and we will respond within the time required by applicable law.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time and will change the effective date at the top of this page when we do. If a change is significant, we will take reasonable steps to let clients know.

12. Contact us

Email ayman@mapdeltahq.com with any question about this policy or your information.
