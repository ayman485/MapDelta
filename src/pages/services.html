Services

The operational work, and its limits.

MapDelta performs the investigation, documentation and follow-up that deduction recovery requires. This page sets out each service, what it produces, and where it stops, so you can judge what you’re buying.

ScopeInitial operating scope: Walmart supplier deductions. Other retailers only after their process has been validated.

Services

01

Deduction analysis

Classification of every deduction in scope by type, amount, age and dispute window.

Output
A classified deduction register and a prioritised view of the backlog.
Limits
Classification starts from the retailer’s reason codes, which are sometimes vague. Where a code doesn’t explain a deduction, we say so rather than guess.
02

Invoice reconciliation

Matching deductions to the invoices and remittances they relate to.

Output
A reconciliation showing invoiced, paid and deducted amounts for each case.
Limits
The result depends on the completeness of the payment and invoice data you provide.
03

Purchase-order matching

Comparing ordered, shipped, received and invoiced quantities and prices.

Output
Line-level differences between what was ordered, delivered and billed.
Limits
Where receiving records are held only by the retailer, a match may not be possible.
04

Supporting-document assessment

Establishing which records exist, which are missing and whether they support a dispute.

Output
An evidence assessment for each case: potentially supportable, awaiting evidence, or valid.
Limits
An assessment is our evidence-based view, not a legal or contractual determination. The retailer makes the decision.
05

Duplicate and discrepancy identification

Finding repeated deductions and differences between agreed and deducted amounts.

Output
Flagged cases, each with the records that show the discrepancy.
Limits
A flag is a reason to look closer, not proof that a deduction is wrong.
06

Claim preparation

Assembling each dispute with its evidence and reasoning, ready for your review.

Output
A dispute package for your approval, with every document referenced.
Limits
We don’t prepare claims the evidence doesn’t support, even when the amount is large.
07

Dispute follow-up

Submitting approved disputes and following them up through the retailer’s process.

Output
A record of every submission, follow-up and retailer response.
Limits
We act only on disputes you approve and only through access you grant. Retailers decide outcomes, and no approval can be guaranteed.
08

Recovery reconciliation

Matching retailer decisions to the payments and credits that actually follow.

Output
Verified recoveries: cash received or credit applied, case by case.
Limits
An approval is not counted as recovered until it appears in your remittances or as an applied credit.
09

Recurring-cause analysis

Reporting the patterns behind repeated deductions.

Output
A monthly summary of recurring causes, with the cases behind each one.
Limits
We identify patterns. Fixing their operational causes, such as shipping or labelling practices, is for your team.

What we don’t do

Clear limits are part of the service.

  • Provide legal, tax or accounting advice.
  • Promise that a retailer will approve a dispute.
  • Treat every deduction as recoverable.
  • Submit a dispute or contact a retailer without your approval.
  • Count approvals as recoveries before cash or credit arrives.
  • Accept financial records through this website’s form.
  • Claim support for retailers whose processes we haven’t validated.
  • Present software as the service. The work is done by people.
