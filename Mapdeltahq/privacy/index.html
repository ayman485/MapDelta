The short version

  • We collect what you give us (name, brokerage, business email and the optional details on the contact form) so we can reply and talk with you.
  • Client records, such as rate confirmations, PODs and invoices, are used only to do the billing work you engaged us for, under the engagement agreement.
  • We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t use advertising trackers or analytics.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how MapDelta (“we”, “us”) handles information when you visit our website, send us an enquiry, or use our services (the “Services”). The Services include the MapDelta Billing Desk for freight brokers and, on request, WIP reporting preparation for contractors (described at mapdeltahq.com/wip).

If you are a client, the written agreement between your company and MapDelta also governs how we handle the records we process for you. Where that agreement conflicts with this policy, the agreement controls.

Information you share with us may be processed in the United States and in other countries where we and our service providers operate.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Enquiries: your name, brokerage or company name, business email and, if you choose to share them, MC number, loads per month, TMS or accounting system, and any message.
  • Correspondence: anything you include when you email or talk with us.

Client records

When you engage us, you give us a limited user login to your systems, such as your TMS and accounting software, and access to a billing inbox or copied email. Through them we may see rate confirmations, bills of lading, proofs of delivery, lumper and detention receipts, invoices, carrier invoices, payables and receivables records, and the names and business contact details of your shippers, carriers and drivers. These are business records and may include personal information about individuals at those companies.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical data when you visit, such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and the time of the request. We use it to keep the site running and secure.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a pilot or discussion.
  • To provide the Services: chasing proofs of delivery, preparing and sending invoices on your behalf, checking carrier invoices against rate confirmations, preparing carrier pay lists for your approval, collections reminders and reporting.
  • To operate, secure and improve our website and Services.
  • To meet legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

We use client records only to perform the Services you have asked for. We do not use them to market to you or to others. We do not hold payment rights, release payments or access your bank account.

4. How client data is handled

We work through the limited logins and inboxes you set up, and we ask for the lowest level of access that lets us do the agreed work. Retention and deletion terms for client data are set out in the engagement agreement. MapDelta does not currently hold SOC 2, ISO 27001 or similar third-party security certifications.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only:

  • With service providers who help us run our business, such as website hosting, form handling, email and file storage. They may use it only to provide services to us. Our contact form is delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) to our email inbox.
  • With your shippers, carriers and factor, to the extent needed to send invoices, upload to portals, submit to your factoring company or follow up on documents, in your company’s name and as you have directed.
  • With people you ask us to involve, such as your accountant or other advisors.
  • For legal reasons, when required by law or to protect our rights, our clients or others.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger or acquisition, subject to this policy.

6. Cookies and third-party services

Our website does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and does not currently use analytics. Its fonts are served from our own website, not from a third party.

If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy first.

7. Retention

We keep enquiries and related correspondence for as long as needed to follow up and for our business records, and generally no longer than 24 months after our last contact with you. We keep client data for the length of the engagement and as set out in the engagement agreement, after which we delete or return it unless we must keep it by law.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information, including access limits and encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including California and other US states with privacy laws, you may have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Get a copy of it.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do neither).
  • Not be treated differently for using these rights.

To make a request, email ayman@mapdeltahq.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding, and we will respond within the time required by applicable law.

10. Children

Our website and Services are for businesses and are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will take reasonable steps to let clients know.

12. Contact us

Email ayman@mapdeltahq.com with any question about this policy or your information.
