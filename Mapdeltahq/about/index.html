About

A small, focused operation that takes responsibility for its work.

MapDelta is founder-led and operated from India, working with consumer-goods suppliers in the United States. We would rather tell you exactly what we are than appear larger than we are.

Who we are

Founder-led, by design.

MapDelta is an early-stage company. The founder leads every engagement, is your direct point of contact, and is accountable for the quality of every case that reaches you.

The service is deliberately narrow at the start. We are building our operating procedures around Walmart supplier deductions first, so that every step, from intake to reconciliation, is tested and documented before the scope widens to other retailers.

How we work

What you can hold us to.

  • 01

    Analytical, record by record

    Each conclusion traces to a source document. If the evidence isn’t there, the case says so.

  • 02

    A structured operation

    The same five stages for every case, with a defined output at each one and nothing skipped for small amounts.

  • 03

    Evidence quality over volume

    We would rather submit fewer, well-supported disputes than many weak ones that damage your standing with the retailer.

  • 04

    A focused initial scope

    We take on a retailer only after confirming its dispute process, evidence requirements and time limits.

  • 05

    Direct accountability

    You deal with the person responsible for the work. Mistakes are reported to you, corrected and recorded.

  • 06

    Honest results

    Only cash received or credit applied counts as recovered, in our reports and in our fees.

Where we operate

Operated from India. Working with US suppliers.

MapDelta is operated from India. We work with suppliers in the United States and aim to reply to enquiries within one US business day.

Because your records may be processed in India, how they are exchanged, stored, retained and returned is agreed in your service agreement before anything is shared. Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle information sent through this website.
