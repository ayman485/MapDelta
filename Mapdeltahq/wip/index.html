The problem we help address

Your monthly WIP should not depend on chasing spreadsheets.

Preparing a reliable WIP schedule often means pulling together information from accounting systems, billing records and project managers. When these inputs are scattered, month-end reporting can become a manual exercise.

01

Fragmented project data

Job costs, billing records, change orders and cost-to-complete estimates live in different places.

02

Unexplained margin movement

A project can appear healthy until estimated costs change. Without a structured review, margin deterioration may go unnoticed.

03

Reconciliation pressure

Overbilling, underbilling and job-level figures need to be understood alongside the general ledger.

MapDelta brings these inputs together into a consistent monthly reporting process.

What we deliver

One structured WIP package. Every month.

  1. Monthly WIP Schedule

    Job-level reporting that consolidates contract value, costs, estimated completion costs, billings and projected profitability.

  2. GL Reconciliation

    A reconciliation workpaper comparing WIP calculations with the relevant general ledger balances, with differences identified and explained where possible.

  3. Margin Fade Analysis

    Highlight projects where projected gross margins have deteriorated and identify movements that require review.

  4. Change-Order Exposure

    Track approved and pending change orders separately so their effect on contract value and project reporting is visible.

  5. PM Follow-up Register

    Identify missing or outdated cost-to-complete estimates and outstanding project-manager inputs.

  6. Monthly Reporting Package

    A structured package designed for internal review and discussion with the contractor’s finance team and relevant external advisors.

How the figures are built. Projected total cost = costs to date + estimated cost to complete. Percentage complete = costs to date ÷ projected total cost (cost-to-cost). Earned revenue = percentage complete × revised contract, rounded to the nearest dollar. Over/(under) billing = billings to date − earned revenue; a positive figure is overbilled, a figure in brackets is underbilled. Projected gross profit = revised contract − projected total cost. Contractors and their accountants choose the method that applies to their own reporting.

How it works

A repeatable month-end process.

  1. 01

    Share the source data

    Your team provides the agreed accounting exports, billing information, change-order records and project estimates through a secure file-sharing process.

  2. 02

    Consolidate the job data

    MapDelta maps and organizes the information into a consistent project-level structure.

  3. 03

    Reconcile and review

    We prepare the WIP calculations, compare relevant balances with the general ledger and flag discrepancies, missing inputs and material margin movements.

  4. 04

    Receive your WIP package

    Your team receives the completed reporting package, open questions and a walkthrough of the key items.

The delivery timeline begins once the agreed source data and required project-manager inputs have been received. Day 10 is a proposed service commitment, not an unconditional guarantee.

FAQ

Questions contractors ask first.

What is a WIP schedule?

A Work in Progress schedule tracks the financial position of active construction projects, including contract values, costs incurred, estimated completion costs, billings and projected profitability.

Who is MapDelta designed for?

Bonded commercial electrical subcontractors, particularly those using QuickBooks or Sage 100 Contractor and maintaining WIP reporting through spreadsheets or manual processes.

Does MapDelta replace our accountant or controller?

No. MapDelta provides WIP preparation, data organization and reconciliation support. It does not replace a qualified construction accountant, controller, CPA or other professional responsible for accounting decisions and financial oversight.

What accounting systems do you support?

We are initially focused on QuickBooks and Sage 100 Contractor. We confirm the exact version, the exports and the available data during the initial discussion. We work from the files your system produces and do not rely on a direct integration.

What information do you need?

Typical inputs include job-cost detail, contract and change-order information, billing registers, a trial balance or relevant GL balances, and project-manager cost-to-complete estimates.

How long does the process take?

The intended monthly delivery target is by business day 10 after month-end, subject to receiving the agreed source data and project-manager inputs on time.

Can you help us rebuild an existing WIP schedule?

Yes. The initial WIP Rebuild engagement is designed to reconstruct a previous month’s WIP package using agreed source data and scope.

Do you provide accounting or surety advice?

No. MapDelta provides reporting preparation and reconciliation support. Accounting judgments, financial statement opinions, tax advice, surety advice and bonding decisions remain with the relevant qualified professionals.
