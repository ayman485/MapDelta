Frequently asked questions

Do you need access to our Amazon or retail accounts?

Not for monitoring or your free snapshot. To file Brand Registry complaints on Growth and Scale, you add us as a user with limited permissions.

Who do the notices come from?

From your brand, using your MAP policy. We send them on your behalf and log every step.

What if we don’t have a MAP policy yet?

We’ll share a policy framework you can review with your legal counsel before enforcement begins.

Can you guarantee sellers will stop?

No one honestly can. We guarantee every violation is found, verified, and acted on, and that you see exactly who complied and who didn’t.

Are you a law firm?

No. We handle monitoring and policy enforcement. Formal legal action goes through your counsel.

How is the free snapshot different from the paid service?

The snapshot is a one-time scan of your top products showing current violations. The service is continuous monitoring plus enforcement.
