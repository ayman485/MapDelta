Frequently asked questions

Do you need access to our Amazon or retail accounts?

Not for monitoring or your free snapshot. On Growth and Scale, if we find genuine IP infringement (counterfeits, copied images, trademark misuse), you can add us as a Brand Registry user with limited permissions so we can report it. A low price alone is never reported as infringement.

Who do the notices come from?

From your brand, using your MAP policy. We send them on your behalf and log every step.

What if we don’t have a MAP policy yet?

We’ll share a policy framework you can review with your legal counsel before enforcement begins.

Can you guarantee sellers will stop?

No one honestly can. What we commit to: every channel you’re on is scanned on schedule, every violation is verified by a person before we act, and you see exactly who complied and who didn’t.

Are you a law firm?

No. We handle monitoring and policy enforcement. Formal legal action goes through your counsel.

How is the free snapshot different from the paid service?

The snapshot is a one-time scan of your top products showing current violations. The service is continuous monitoring plus enforcement.

We already use a monitoring tool or vendor. Why talk to you?

Send us your top products and we’ll check them for free. If we find violations your current process missed, you’ll see them. If we don’t, you’ve lost nothing.

How do you avoid flagging our own dealers by mistake?

You share your authorized dealer list at onboarding, and a person checks every flagged listing against it before any notice is sent.

What happens with sellers who ignore notices?

They move up a set escalation ladder. For persistent unauthorized sellers, we trace where their stock comes from so you can close the source, and we coordinate with your counsel if formal action is needed.

Where is your team based?

MapDelta is run from Hyderabad, India, and works with US brands. We reply within one US business day.
