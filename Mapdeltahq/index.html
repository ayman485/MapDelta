MapDelta Billing Desk for freight brokers

Every delivered load, invoiced within one business day.

MapDelta runs the billing desk for small freight brokerages. We chase the POD, build the invoice packet, bill the shipper and line up carrier pay, inside your own TMS. You keep booking freight.

Start a two-week pilot

Pilot: up to 75 loads for $400, credited to your first month.

The problem

Billing should not wait until your night shift.

Loads deliver all day. PODs sit in a driver’s camera roll, invoices go out a week late, and a $150 detention charge never makes it onto the bill. Every late invoice is cash you’ve already paid a carrier and haven’t collected.

What we deliver

One billing desk, every business day.

  • POD chased the day the load delivers
  • Invoice packet built: rate con, BOL, POD, receipts
  • Accessorials checked against the rate confirmation
  • Shipper invoiced, or uploaded to your factor, within one business day
  • Carrier invoices matched to the rate con before you pay
  • Collections follow-up at 30 and 45 days

What we don’t touch: booking loads, carrier sales, rate negotiation, releasing payments, or your bank account. We prepare the carrier pay list. You approve and release every payment.

FAQ

Questions brokers ask first.

Do you touch our money?

No. We prepare the carrier pay list; you approve and release every payment.

Do you talk to our shippers?

Only for billing: sending invoices, portal uploads and payment reminders, in your company’s name and tone.

We use a factoring company.

Fine. We build the packet and submit it to your factor.

What hours do you work?

US business hours, Monday to Friday.

Can we cancel?

Yes, with 14 days’ notice.
