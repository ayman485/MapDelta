Our evidence standard

What we hold ourselves to.

  1. Every flag has a timestamp and a screenshot.

    If we can’t show you the listing as it appeared, we don’t report it.

  2. A person checks every flag against your dealer list before it reaches you.

    Automated matching gets bundles, used items and your own dealers wrong often enough to matter.

FAQ

Questions

Do you need access to our Amazon or retail accounts?

Not for monitoring or your free snapshot. On Growth and Scale, if we find genuine IP infringement (counterfeits, copied images, trademark misuse), you can add us as a Brand Registry user with limited permissions so we can report it. A low price alone is never reported as infringement.

Who do the notices come from?

From your brand, under your MAP policy, using templates your counsel approves. We send them on your behalf only after you approve, and we log every step.

What if we don’t have a MAP policy yet?

We’ll share a policy framework you can review with your counsel before anything is sent.

Can you guarantee sellers will come back to MAP?

No. No one honestly can. What we commit to: every channel in your plan is checked on schedule, every flag is checked by a person before anything is sent, and you see exactly who changed their price and who didn’t.

Are you a law firm?

No. We handle monitoring, verification and policy follow-up. Formal legal action goes through your counsel.

How do you keep your own dealers from being flagged by mistake?

You share your authorized dealer list at onboarding, and a person checks every flagged listing against it before it reaches you. Anything we can’t match comes to you as a flag to verify, never as a notice.

How quickly do you reply?

Within one US business day, and your snapshot arrives within 48 hours of your request.
