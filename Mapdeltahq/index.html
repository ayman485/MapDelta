02 — The problem

Deductions don’t stop arriving because your finance team is busy.

  1. 01Repeated deductions from the same retailers, cycle after cycle.
  2. 02Claims that stay open because nobody has time to close them.
  3. 03Supporting documents spread across portals, inboxes and shared drives.
  4. 04Invoice reconciliation that takes longer than the deduction seems worth.
  5. 05Follow-ups that slip once a dispute has been submitted.
  6. 06Small claims deprioritised until their dispute window closes.
  7. 07Discrepancies that are noticed but never investigated.

Some deductions may be incorrect or disputable. Others may be valid, incomplete or unsupported. The work is finding out which, and that is exactly the work that accumulates.

03 — The MapDelta approach

From unexplained deduction to documented financial outcome.

  1. 01

    Stage 1: Capture

    We receive the deduction and payment information you authorise us to use: retailer deduction reports, remittance detail and the period in scope.

    OutputA complete intake register of deductions in scope.

  2. 02

    Stage 2: Investigate

    We classify each deduction by type and reconcile it against your payment and invoice records, so every line has a reason code and a known history.

    OutputClassified deductions, reconciled to remittances.

  3. 03

    Stage 3: Validate

    We match invoices, purchase orders, agreements and supporting documents to the deduction, and assess whether the evidence supports a dispute.

    OutputAn evidence assessment for each case: supportable, incomplete or valid.

  4. 04

    Stage 4: Resolve

    We prepare evidence-backed disputes for your approval. Once you approve, we submit them and manage the follow-up you have authorised through the retailer’s process.

    OutputClient-approved disputes, submitted and followed up.

  5. 05

    Stage 5: Reconcile

    We track every retailer decision and verify the outcome against your actual remittances and credits. A case counts as recovered only when the cash is received or the credit is applied.

    OutputVerified recoveries and a monthly report.

11 — Questions

What finance teams ask first.

01What is retailer deduction recovery?

Retailers often pay suppliers less than the invoiced amount, deducting for reasons such as shortages, pricing differences, promotional allowances or compliance chargebacks. Recovery is the work of investigating those deductions, establishing which may be incorrect or unsupported, disputing them with evidence through the retailer’s process, and tracking each case until money is received, a credit is applied, or the case is closed.

02What kinds of deductions can MapDelta investigate?

Common types include shortages, pricing and cost differences, promotional and allowance deductions, compliance or operational chargebacks, damage and return deductions, and duplicate deductions. Whether a particular deduction is worth investigating depends on its type, the retailer’s rules and the records you hold. If a category is outside what we can investigate well, we’ll tell you.

03Which retailers do you currently support?

Our initial operating scope is Walmart supplier deductions. If you sell to other retailers, tell us. We only take on another retailer’s deductions once we have confirmed its dispute process, evidence requirements and time limits, and we’ll be clear about that before any work starts.

04How does an engagement begin?

With a conversation about your deduction process and backlog. If an initial investigation makes sense, we agree a defined scope (retailer, period and approximate record volume) and the fees in a written service agreement before any records are shared.

05What records will you need?

Typically: retailer deduction and remittance reports, invoices, purchase orders, proof of delivery and carrier records, and relevant agreements such as promotional or allowance terms. The exact list depends on the deduction types in scope, and we confirm it with you at the start.

06Do you submit disputes?

Yes, when you authorise it. We prepare each dispute with its evidence, you approve it, and we submit and follow it up through the retailer’s process using access you grant for that purpose. Nothing is submitted without your approval.

07Does every deduction represent recoverable money?

No. Many deductions are valid under your agreements or the retailer’s terms. Others may be disputable but lack the evidence needed to support a claim. Part of the work is telling you which is which, so effort goes where there is a supportable case.

08How do you calculate performance fees?

The performance fee is a percentage of eligible recoveries that are attributable to our work and actually realised: cash received or credit applied, as defined in your service agreement. It does not apply to deductions identified, disputes submitted, or approvals not yet paid or credited. The percentage and the monthly fee are agreed in writing before work begins.

09What happens when a dispute is denied?

We record the retailer’s reason and assess whether further evidence, or a review available within the retailer’s process, could reasonably change the outcome. Then we recommend whether to continue or close the case, and you decide. A denied case carries no performance fee.

10How is client data handled?

Records are exchanged after engagement through a secure method agreed with you, never through this website’s form. Access is limited to what the agreed scope requires. How your records are stored, how long they are kept, and how they are returned or deleted are set out in the service agreement. We don’t claim security certifications we don’t hold.

11Where is MapDelta based?

MapDelta is operated from India and works with suppliers in the United States. We aim to reply to enquiries within one US business day.

12Who performs the work?

MapDelta is founder-led. The founder leads every engagement and is your direct point of contact. If anyone else works on your cases, we’ll tell you who they are and what they have access to before they start.

13How is work reviewed?

Each case is checked against its source documents before it reaches you, and you review and approve every dispute before it is submitted. Recoveries are reconciled against your actual remittances and credits, not against approvals. MapDelta does not currently use independent third-party reviewers.

14Can MapDelta work alongside an existing recovery provider?

Often, yes, provided the scopes are clearly separated (for example by retailer, deduction type or period), so the same deduction is never disputed twice. We agree that boundary with you in writing before starting.

15What happens when a claim is partially approved?

The case stays open until the approved amount is actually received or credited. Only that realised amount counts as recovered and is eligible for a performance fee. We record the unapproved balance and recommend whether any of it is worth pursuing further.
